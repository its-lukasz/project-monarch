Every item ends the same way: what comes next.

How it works

  1. 1. Tell us your tools.Microsoft 365, Slack, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Okta, whatever your company runs on. Costs and renewal dates if you have them.
  2. 2. Monarch reads the release notes and price pages.For each of your tools, each week, and checks every change against the plan you’re on.
  3. 3. You see what to do, and by when.What’s new in your plan, sorted by team. Where you can save, with a dollar range, an owner, and a date to act by. A weekly email every Monday.

New in the plans you pay for

Vendors add features to plans you already have, change what each plan includes, and raise prices. Monarch checks every change against your plan and tells each team what is now included, what needs a higher plan, and what costs more.

Do we already pay for this?

The tool your team is about to buy may already be in something you pay for. Monarch checks each new feature against the rest of your tools and tells you before you buy twice.

Know what to decide before you renew.

Seats nobody uses, two tools doing one job, monthly plans that cost more than annual. Monarch shows each one with an estimated dollar range, who owns the tool, and the date to act by, so nothing renews by default.

You may already pay for AI you aren’t using.

Most of the tools you pay for now include AI features. Monarch shows which ones are in your plan, what each team could use them for, and how to switch them on.

No vendor pays us.

Monarch doesn’t sell software, take referral fees, or run sponsored posts. That’s why we can tell you when a feature you already own makes another subscription unnecessary.

Every vendor writes its own release notes and sends its own invoice. Monarch puts what changed and what it costs in one list.
