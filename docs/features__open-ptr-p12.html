Turn on Zoom AI Companion summaries

Zoom Workplace
May replace Otter.aiIncluded in your planSalesOperations
What it does

Zoom Pro includes unlimited AI Companion meeting summaries at no extra cost.

Who gets it
Account owners and admins turn this on. Meeting hosts then start it in their meetings.1
Plan
Needs a paid Zoom Workplace plan: Pro, Pro Plus, Business, Business Plus, Enterprise, Enterprise Plus or Enterprise Bundle.1Zoom Workplace Pro, Zoom Workplace Pro Plus, Zoom Workplace Business, Zoom Workplace Business Plus, Zoom Workplace Enterprise, Zoom Workplace Enterprise Plus, or Enterprise Bundle account

Before you start
  • You need to be the account owner, an admin, or hold a custom role that can change account settings.1
  • Zoom Rooms users need Zoom Rooms version 5.16.0 or higher.2
Turn it on: for your admin
  1. Sign in to the Zoom web portal as an admin who can edit account settings.1
  2. In the Zoom web portal, click your profile picture or initials in the top-right corner, then click Admin Center.1
  3. In the Admin Center side menu, click Settings.1
  4. On the Zoom AI page, under Meeting, click the Meeting summary with AI toggle to turn it on.1
  5. If a verification dialog appears, click Enable to confirm the change.1
  6. Under Automatically share summary with, choose who gets summaries automatically.1
